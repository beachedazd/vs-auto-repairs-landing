# Square invoice template — VS Auto Repairs

Square's invoice branding/template layer is Dashboard-only (no API). Owner setup, ~5 min.

## 1. Branding — Account & Settings → Business information → Branding
- Colour: `#12314F` (site navy)
- Upload shop logo (square-ish PNG)

## 2. Invoice defaults — Invoices → Settings → Invoice preferences
- Default title: `Tax Invoice`
- Staff habit per job: `Tax Invoice — <job> — <rego>` e.g. "Tax Invoice — RWC — ABC123"
- Payment terms: Due on receipt (fleet/account customers: change per-invoice)
- Accepted methods: Card + Bank transfer
- Reminders: day before due, on due date, 3 days after

Thanks for choosing VS Auto Repairs. This invoice covers the vehicle shown in the title. Total includes GST.

## 4. Tax-invoice compliance
- Enter ABN 35 160 506 650 in Account & Settings → Business information (this is what
  makes Square's invoices legal *tax invoices*)
- GST prints automatically — catalog items are GST-inclusive via the built-in AU GST
