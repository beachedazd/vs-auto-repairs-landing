Straight answers

How long will you have the car?

Drop off between 8:30 and 10am, collect from 4pm. Most services and roadworthies are done that day. If it's a bigger job, you get the price and the day before we start.

What happens if the roadworthy fails?

You get the written list of what needs fixing, with a price to fix each item here. Fix it here or anywhere you like, bring it back within the re-test window for a re-inspection, not a full test again.

Will servicing here void my new-car warranty?

No. A log book service done to the manufacturer's schedule, with parts that meet the manufacturer's spec, keeps your warranty. We stamp the book. You don't have to go back to the dealer.

Why you and not the chain up the road?

We're one workshop on Colemans Rd, same owner since 2008. You get the price before we start, and when you ring, the workshop picks up. Read the reviews and decide.

Do I need to book?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp with the rego and we'll give you the next free morning. Something urgent like a brake noise? Say so, and we'll fit it in as early as we can.

Utes, 4WDs, light trucks, EVs?

Yes. We service and repair them every day, and we're licensed to roadworthy EVs and light vehicles up to 4.5 tonnes.
